# Name and logo

The MIT License in [LICENSE](LICENSE) covers the code, documentation, textures and other files of
ContainerUI, **except** the name and the logo described here.

"ContainerUI" and the ContainerUI logo identify this project and its author. They are not licensed
under the MIT License. The logo is the files `assets/logo.svg`, `assets/logo-dark.svg`,
`assets/avatar.svg` and `assets/avatar.png`, the copies in the `logo/` folder that ships with the
library, and the pack icons `pack_icon.png` in the ContainerUI packs.

## You may

- Say that your add-on is built with, uses or is compatible with ContainerUI, in text, and link to
  the repository.
- Show the logo, unchanged, to say so (for example next to "Built with ContainerUI"), as long as it
  does not look like your own project's logo or like an endorsement.
- Redistribute the library with its `logo/` folder, and the ContainerUI packs with their pack icons,
  unchanged.
- Use the name to describe the unmodified library: in articles, tutorials, videos and package lists.

## You may not

- Use the name or the logo as the name or logo of your own project, product, server or add-on.
- Name a modified version or a fork "ContainerUI", or something likely to be confused with it, such as
  "ContainerUI Pro" or "ContainerUI 2", or use the logo for it. A fork chooses its own name and logo;
  its code can stay MIT, with the copyright notice kept.
- Change the logo and present it as ContainerUI's.
- Suggest that your project is official, endorsed by or made by the author when it is not.
